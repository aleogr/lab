aleogr-lab-shared-dacd:us-central1:lab-postgres — one PostgreSQL 16 instance, one database per project.

It sleeps four weeknights — Monday to Thursday, 22:00–07:30 (UTC−3) — and is awake the rest of the time, Friday night and the whole weekend included. Inside that window a project's card reads down or unknown, and both are expected: when a project cannot answer for itself, the platform in front of it answers instead, and this page cannot read that answer. Outside the window, either one means something is actually wrong.
